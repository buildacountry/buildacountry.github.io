Vinland (Build a Country): privacy

Vinland is a free online game run by the Instagram page @buildacountry. Every follower gets a home on a 3D map at buildacountry.github.io. This page explains what we use and what we never use.

What we use

  • Your public Instagram username, to put your name on your home on the map.
  • Comments you leave on our posts and messages you send us, so we can build your home, reply to you and record requests (a job, a shop, an idea).
  • Game details we create for you: your home number, district, job and the day you joined.

What we never collect or sell

  • We never ask for your real name, age, birthday, phone number, email or address.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for ads.

Walking on the map

When you use walk mode, your position is shared live with other walkers as an anonymous "Visitor". It is not stored.

Removing your data

Send "DELETE MY HOME" as a message to @buildacountry on Instagram. We remove your username from the map and our records within 7 days. Unfollowing the page also marks your home as empty.

Contact

Message @buildacountry on Instagram.
